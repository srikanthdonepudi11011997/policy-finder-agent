Policy: Travel Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Status: Approved
Owner: Finance

Employees may book premium economy for flights over 6 hours. Economy is required for shorter flights.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 220 USD per night. All trips need manager approval at least 7 days before departure.

Per diem for meals is 75 USD per day.
